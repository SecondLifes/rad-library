# RAD Library AI Spec-Kit — Image Prompts

## Art direction — “The Blueprint Loom”

A quiet architectural paper-theatre where a hand-operated loom weaves precise
Delphi-blueprint ribbons into small reusable mechanisms. No robots, computers,
foundries, prisms, flames or libraries. Palette: chalk white, vermilion and
moss green on warm sand. Style: layered cut-paper relief with visible fibers,
soft side light and restrained technical annotations. The loom and woven
modules are the shared metaphor for a small core plus optional integrations.

## README banner

Wide 3:1 cut-paper relief. A compact wooden blueprint loom centered slightly

component and optional vendor module. Chalk white, vermilion, moss green,
warm-sand background; tactile paper fibers, soft side shadows, generous empty
space for the title “RAD Library AI Spec-Kit”. No logos or vendor marks.
